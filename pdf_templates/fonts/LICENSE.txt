DejaVu Sans (Regular and Bold) is bundled here so PDF output renders
correctly — including the ₹ (Indian Rupee) symbol, which the standard 14
PDF base fonts (Helvetica, Times, Courier) do not include at all — no
matter what fonts happen to be installed on the machine running the app.

DejaVu fonts are released under a free, permissive license (based on the
Bitstream Vera license) that explicitly allows embedding and
redistribution, with or without modification. Full license text:
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html
